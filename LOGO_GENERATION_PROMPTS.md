# Logo Generation Prompts for Cashual

Based on your application's branding, design theme, and functionality, here are optimized prompts you can use with AI image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, etc.) to create logos for Cashual.

## Brand Context Summary
- **Name**: Cashual
- **Industry**: FinTech / Accounting Software
- **Target Audience**: Canadian incorporated contractors and small businesses
- **Core Features**: Accounting, Payroll, Tax Compliance (CRA), Employee Management
- **Brand Personality**: Professional, trustworthy, modern, approachable, affordable
- **Design Theme**: FinTech Luxury - dark mode, sophisticated, glassmorphism aesthetic
- **Design Direction**: **Ultra-minimalist and posh** - clean, refined, elegant, premium

- **Background**: Deep Forest (#020402)

## Recommended Approach

1. **Start with Option 1** (Ultra-Minimalist Posh Icon) - Best for minimalist luxury aesthetic
2. **Focus on simplicity** - Less is more - aim for single element, maximum negative space
3. **Test on dark backgrounds** - Your app uses dark mode, so ensure the logo works well on `#020402`
4. **Consider scalability** - Logo should work from favicon (16x16) to large banners - minimalism helps here
5. **Maintain elegance** - Use single color (neon emerald), avoid gradients for true minimalism
6. **Think luxury brands** - Reference Apple, Tesla, Hermès, Chanel for inspiration on minimalist luxury

---

## Post-Generation Tips

After generating your logo:
1. **Test in your navbar** - Replace the current "C" icon with your new logo
2. **Create favicon versions** - Generate 16x16, 32x32, 192x192, 512x512 sizes
3. **Test contrast** - Ensure logo is visible on both dark backgrounds and light surfaces
4. **Get feedback** - Share with potential users (Canadian contractors) for feedback
5. **Create variations** - Consider horizontal and vertical layouts for different use cases
